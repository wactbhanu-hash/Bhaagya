# भाग्य — Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: 2026-08-09

भाग्य processes information entered by the user, such as name, birth date/time and birth location, to calculate and display a traditional astrology chart.

The current architecture does not require sending birth information to a third-party server for basic calculations.

Before Play Store publication, the publisher must replace this draft with a final legally reviewed privacy policy at a public URL and add a support contact.
