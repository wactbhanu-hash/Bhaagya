# भाग्य V5 — Evidence / Source Policy

## Goal
The prediction engine must be traceable. Every automated interpretation needs a source record.

## Required fields before a rule can be published
- Rule ID
- Rule title
- Tradition/school
- Exact source title
- Specific edition/translation
- Exact locator (chapter/verse/page/section as applicable)
- The original source text or a legally usable paraphrase
- The implemented logical condition
- Interpretation
- Limitations

## Evidence levels
### PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED
The source has been checked against a specific edition and the locator is recorded.

### SECONDARY_SOURCE
A modern scholarly/secondary source is used. The app must label it as secondary.

### USER_DEFINED
A rule entered by the user. It is never presented as an ancient or independently verified rule.

## No fabricated citations
The app must never invent a verse number, quote, chapter, page, or claim that a rule comes from an ancient text without verification.

## AI policy
AI can:
- explain a matched rule in simple language;
- summarize the source-backed conclusion;
- compare multiple matched rules;
- clearly state uncertainty.

AI cannot:
- invent a source;
- invent a verse;
- silently add a new astrological rule;
- convert a traditional interpretation into a guaranteed scientific prediction.

## Product wording
Avoid claims such as:
- "100% accurate future"
- "scientifically proven astrology"
- "guaranteed wealth/marriage prediction"

Prefer:
- "source-backed traditional interpretation"
- "astronomical calculation"
- "interpretation confidence / evidence level"
