## Sources located for the first evidence corpus

### Bṛhat Jātaka — Varāhamihira
A 1905 edition is catalogued by Open Library and linked to an Internet Archive item. The edition is described as Madras, Printed by Thompson & Co., 1905. It is useful as a historical/public-domain research edition, but the exact edition and Sanskrit base text must be checked before a rule is labeled PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED.

https://openlibrary.org/books/OL13997226M/The_Brihat_jataka

### Bṛhat Saṃhitā — Varāhamihira
SanskritDocuments provides scans and Sanskrit/translation resources for Bṛhat Saṃhitā. These resources can help with research and cross-checking, but the commercial app should store only text that is legally usable and should preserve source attribution.

## Verification status
No rule is promoted to PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED merely because a book exists online. A rule must have:
1. identifiable source edition;
2. exact chapter/verse/section locator;
3. source text checked against that edition;
4. a legally usable representation for the app;
5. implementation reviewed against the source meaning.

## Important commercial-rights note
The existence of a scan online does not automatically grant commercial reuse rights. The app should store citations/metadata and use public-domain or properly licensed text/translations only.
